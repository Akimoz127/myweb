Who We Are

AI Learning Stream is a free educational platform created by Ahmed. The goal is simple: make learning Artificial Intelligence easier, clearer, and accessible to everyone.

Our Mission

This platform organizes AI videos into categories, trending lists, watch-later collections, and more. It is designed with a modern neon-dark interface and built as a Progressive Web App (PWA) so you can install it on your device.

About Ahmed

Ahmed is passionate about AI, web development, and building useful tools for learners. This project is part of his journey to create a complete AI learning hub that grows over time.
